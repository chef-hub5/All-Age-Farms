About All Age Farms

Company History

All-Age Farms was registered in 2023 with one goal: to solve the trust problem in Nigeria's smoked protein market.

We started this business seeing how much good smoked fish and meat was wasted or rejected because of poor hygiene, sand, and inconsistent quality. In Lagos kitchens and export containers alike, customers were paying for "clean" but getting stress.

So we built All-Age Farms differently. From day one, we invested in a controlled processing facility, trained our staff on food safety standards, and got a drying system that dries products slowly and keeps products fresh longer without chemicals.

What started as a small Lagos operation serving families has grown into a trusted supplier for restaurants, food distributors, and export clients across Nigeria and West Africa. Today, All-Age Farms is known for one thing: clean, consistent, traditionally and properly smoked protein that meets global standards.

Our Mission

To provide safe, hygienic, and consistently high-quality smoked and dried protein to homes, restaurants, and export markets — while preserving the authentic Nigerian taste that people love.

We don't just sell food. We deliver peace of mind with every pack.

Our Values

1. Cleanliness First

Hygiene isn't optional for us. From processing to packaging, every step meets strict food safety standards because what we produce ends up in someone's pot.

2. Tradition with Integrity

We respect the traditional smoking methods that give Nigerian food its unique flavor. But we combine it with modern standards so you get both taste and safety.

3. Consistency You Can Trust

Whether you're ordering 2kg or 200kg, your product will look, taste, and arrive the same way. No surprises.

4. Customer Respect

We treat every order like it's for our own family. That means honest communication, fair pricing, and on-time delivery.

5. Rooted, Not Stuck

We honor our Nigerian roots while staying open to global standards and export opportunities. That's how we serve both local and international customers.

Return & Refund Policy

1. Our Commitment

At All Age Farms, we stand behind the quality of every product we deliver. We are committed to ensuring that you receive fresh, hygienic, and properly processed products every time. If your order does not meet our standards, we will make it right.

2. Eligibility for Returns & Refunds

Returns and refunds are available for orders that arrive:

  • Damaged or spoiled: Product shows signs of spoilage, contamination, mold, or pest damage upon delivery
  • Not as described: Product received does not match the order or product description (wrong item, wrong quantity, wrong size/grade)
  • Defective: Product has visible defects not present in product images (excessive moisture, burning, foreign matter, etc.)

Note: Natural variations in product appearance due to traditional smoking methods are not grounds for return. We do not accept returns for change of mind or personal preference after inspection and acceptance of delivery.

3. Return Process

Step 1: Report Within 24 Hours

Contact us within 24 hours of receiving your order via WhatsApp or email if you believe your order is damaged, spoiled, or not as described.

Step 2: Provide Evidence

Send clear photos or video of:

  • The damaged or defective product
  • The original packaging (if damaged in transit)
  • Any visible defects, mold, or contamination

Step 3: We Will Verify

Our team will review your evidence and determine if the return qualifies. This typically takes 24-48 hours.

Step 4: Resolution

If your claim is approved, we will either:

  • Replace the product at no cost (shipped at our expense)
  • Issue a full refund to your original payment method
  • Offer a store credit for future orders

4. Refund Timeline

Once a return is approved:

  • Bank Transfer Refunds: 3-5 business days to appear in your account
  • Pay-On-Delivery Refunds: Will be refunded via bank transfer within 5 business days of approval
  • Replacements: New product will be shipped within 2 business days at no cost

5. Return Shipping

For Replacements: All Age Farms covers the cost of shipping the replacement product to you.

For Refunds: You are not required to return the damaged or defective product. We do not require physical return shipment for refunds. Simply provide photographic evidence of the issue.

6. Non-Returnable Items

The following are not eligible for returns or refunds:

  • Orders accepted and stored improperly by the customer
  • Orders partially consumed or tampered with
  • Products damaged due to customer negligence after delivery
  • Orders placed in error or change of mind (no defect)
  • Claims reported more than 24 hours after delivery

7. Storage & Care Instructions

To ensure product freshness and avoid spoilage after delivery:

  • Refrigerate: Store smoked fish and meat in the refrigerator (0-4°C) for up to 2 weeks
  • Freeze: For longer storage, freeze in airtight containers for up to 3 months
  • Dry Storage: Flour and dried pap should be stored in cool, dry places away from moisture and pests
  • Check Before Storage: Inspect products immediately upon delivery before placing in storage

8. Bulk Orders

For bulk orders (above 20kg), we recommend inspecting products in the presence of our delivery agent. Any issues must be reported immediately and documented with photos before the agent leaves.

9. Exceptions & Special Cases

In rare cases where circumstances warrant special consideration (e.g., delivery failure, major contamination, multiple defects), we encourage customers to reach out. We will work with you to find a fair resolution.
